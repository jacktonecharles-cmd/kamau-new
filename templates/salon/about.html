Company Story

Crafted with Care, Since Day One

Kamau Nails began as a single chair with a simple promise: treat every client like royalty. Today we're a full luxury studio, but that promise hasn't changed — every appointment is an experience, not just a service.

Our mission is to make world-class nail artistry accessible, hygienic and joyful. Our vision is to become the most trusted nail destination in the region, known for creativity and consistency in equal measure.
